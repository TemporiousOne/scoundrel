The Room

How to Play

Goal: Survive the entire dungeon deck.

  • Weapons (♦): Equip to fight monsters. Cannot attack a monster stronger than the last one you killed.
  • Potions (♥): Restores health. You can only use one per room.
  • Monsters (♣/♠): Fight barehanded (take full damage) or with a weapon (take damage = monster val - weapon val).

Fleeing: You can escape an untouched room [E], cards will be moved to bottom of deck.

URL Parameters:

  • health - sets your starting health
  • escapes - number of times you can escape consecutively
  • invincible - starts with BFG9000 😁
  • ltoe - Uses ≤ when comparing weapon stat with monster. Set to 0 to use <.
  • Example: ?health=99&escapes=10&invincible=1

Extras:

  • Press "+" to get a health boost.
